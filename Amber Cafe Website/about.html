The Vibrant Our Roots

We founded Velvet & Amber in 2018 with a strong desire to destroy the boring, sterile white cafe aesthetic. We wanted color, texture, flavor explosion, and late-night passion.

Our coffee is directly traded from high-altitude micro-lots in Colombia and Ethiopia. We pay premium rates directly to farm pickers to guarantee absolute perfection in sustainability and raw crop choices.

Step inside our colorful bubble, plug in your laptop, or sit and enjoy a deep laugh with a stranger. This space is yours.
